# Usance Brand Lock — Capacity Cut

## Brand thesis
Usance turns a tokenized asset into usable capital only after evidence-bound admission and deterministic risk policy.

## Chosen mark
`Capacity Cut`

The large block is the registered asset / legal-economic record.
The missing section is the portion that is not automatically usable.
The detached module is the capacity that becomes usable only after admission.
The visible gap is the evidence/risk boundary.

The mark therefore encodes:

`ASSET → VERIFIED ADMISSION → USABLE CAPACITY`

It is not a letter U, shield, lock, chain link, cube, node graph, token, or generic crypto mark.

## Geometry
- Structural master: `64 × 64`
- One-colour master first
- Main record: left block
- Capacity module: detached right block aligned to the cut
- Gap must always remain visible
- Clear space: minimum `0.5×` the mark width
- Minimum digital size: 16px using `usance-micro-mark.svg`
- Use full mark at 32px and above
- Horizontal lockup minimum: 104px wide

## Palette
- Aubergine Ink `#1b0624` — primary identity
- Espresso `#160f0c` — dark interactive / dark-field alternative
- Linen `#f7f7f5` — default light field
- Paper White `#ffffff` — reversed mark

## Wordmark
Outlined from the approved Inter fallback direction so production SVGs contain paths rather than a font dependency. No font files are included in this kit.

## System potential
The same capacity-cut geometry is reused across:
- Passport
- recognized collateral
- RiskEpoch changes
- liquidation
- mandate authority
- LP vaults
- intent reservations
- proof receipts

## Forbidden
- no blue/green brand accents
- no gradients in the identity mark
- no glow
- no 3D/bevel
- no skew or rotation
- no shield/circle enclosure except app/social containers
- no random U replacement
- no hero watercolor colours inside operational UI or mark
- do not close the gap between asset and capacity
- do not recolour operational SVGs
